CamperBot - AI Chatbot

CamperBot is an AI chatbot built to help users with camping information and guidance.

Overview

It allows users to interact naturally and get helpful camping advice and suggestions.

Features

  • Camping topic responses
  • Chat log export system
  • Expandable AI logic

Technology Stack

Python, NLTK, TensorFlow
